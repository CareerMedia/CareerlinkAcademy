# CareerLink Assistant Academy Module Plan

## Module 1: Welcome to CareerLink

- Activity type: Welcome video to watch
- Purpose: This video will introduce the Career Center, and the position the student will have. It will be simple and short.  
- Estimated time: ~2-5 mins 
- What the learner should understand: The learner must be able to understand their role/title and be have a sense of understanding as to what they will be doing/ what the role wants from them. 
- How the learner completes it: The learner will simply click play and watch the video to the end. Once watched, the learner can click complete and that would satistfy the requirement. I was thinking of adding a lock such that the learner is not able to click complete module until the video has been fully played at least once. If this is too complex for a prodotype, we will discard this idea for now.
- Accessibility needs: Captions, text on module page must be accessible using a screen reader.

## Module 2: Basic Workplace Setup

- Activity type: Video to watch
- Purpose: This module will contain a video. The video will feature basic programs and tabs to always keep available on your work desktop. These tabs will be specific to what tasks are being performed for the day. E.g, you must have Screaming frog (web crawler) on your PC before working on broken links.
- Estimated time: ~2-3 mins
- What the learner should understand: How to keep their workplace to maintain easy workflow and access. 
- How the learner completes it: Similar to module 1.
- Accessibility needs: Captions, text on module page must be readable by a screen reader.

## Module 3: Familiarize Yourself with CareerLink

- Activity type: Scavanger hunt but as a quiz 
- Purpose: Prompt learner to visit and explore CareerLink. Once ready, they can continue and start the quiz which will ask  10-15 scanvenger style questions. At the end, the score will need to be 80% or above in order for learner to complete the module. This is to incentivise learner to look well into the actual product in order to answer right. 
- Estimated time: unlimited/as much as the learner needs. Save quiz progress if incomplete.
- What the learner should understand: What is contained on the CareerLink site, where to find resources, and what resources are available on CareerLink.
- How the learner completes it: Gets 80% or above in the quiz. 
- Accessibility needs: Text on module page must be readable by a screen reader.

## Module 4: What we do (open to ideas for module name)

- Activity type: Video to watch 
- Purpose: This video will give a rundown on what the daily tasks may look like. It will show what you will work on most, which projects may be monthly (like fixing broken links), which may be throughout the semester (like updating academic programs tab), or which may be at special requests.
- Estimated time: 5-10 mins
- What the learner should understand: What they are actually supposed to do on a daily basis during their role, which tasks are at hand.
- How the learner completes it: Completes by watching the video and clicking complete.
- Accessibility needs: Captions, text on module page must be readable by a screen reader.

## Module 5: Events Tab

- Activity type: Video + prompt at the end to try out the task. 
- Purpose: How to add and edit events tab items. This video will give a rundown of what the events tab is, why we use it and how to edit it. It will show a full guide of the steps to take from beginning to end to add a new event on the website. 
- Estimated time: 5-10 mins
- What the learner should understand: The learner should understand how to add/edit events tab.
- How the learner completes it: The learner will complete the video, and then be prompted to try it out for themselves with an example event to add on the website. The only thing they must keep in mind is to unpublish whatever they make once they have successfully built the example.
- Accessibility needs: Captions, text on module page must be readable by a screen reader.

## Module 6: Academic Programs - Chapter I

- Activity type: Video to watch
- Purpose: This video will teach the overview about what academic program update is. It will show an example program (which will be an actual existing program), and breakdown what each part means.
- Estimated time: ~5 mins
- What the learner should understand: What this academic program updating is, and how to navigate it.
- How the learner completes it: Watches video then clicks complete. 
- Accessibility needs: Captions, text on module page must be readable by a screen reader.

## Module 7: Academic Programs - Chapter II 

- Activity type: Video + prompt
- Purpose: This video will give a guide on how to actually build a new academic program from start. It will show which resources (e.g program catalog) to have in hand, and where to find the important descriptions to add in the program. 
- Estimated time: ~10 mins
- What the learner should understand: The learner must have a basis of where to find the important information to build a academic program from start, what to add, what to research, and how to present it for approval from supervisor. 
- How the learner completes it: Watches video, clicks continue, and the clicks complete once prompt has been read. The trial example must be an actual program and must be reviewed by a supervisor before publishing. 
- Accessibility needs: Captions, text on module page must be readable by a screen reader.

## Module 8: Understanding CareerLink Taxonomy 

- Activity type: Video to watch 
- Purpose: This video will teach where to find taxonomy used in the website, where we can edit it, why it is used, and how to add new taxonomy
- Estimated time: ~2-4 mins
- What the learner should understand: Why taxonmy is important on the website, and how to use it to link new resources to the correct page. 
- How the learner completes it: Watches video then clicks complete. 
- Accessibility needs: Captions, text on module page must be readable by a screen reader.

## Module 9: CareerLink Articles 

- Activity type: Video to watch
- Purpose: This video will give a breakdown of one of the largest parts of CareerLink, the articles. Articles would mean tabs linking to either text information, or links to external sites. The articles tab is used to store external resources, describe them a little more, or simply write about them directly on CareerLink. The video will show how to update these, how to create new articles, when to create new articles, and how to link external sources.
- Estimated time: ~10-15 mins
- What the learner should understand: The learner will be working a lot on updating current article pages and adding new ones at supervisor or staff requests. This video is intended to fimiliarize them with all the basics they may need to know in order to get started with building on the articles. It is not intended to be a full deep guide as to everything the site allows us to do but will let them have just enough knowledge to start building immediately. 
- How the learner completes it: Watches video then clicks complete. 
- Accessibility needs: Captions, text on module page must be readable by a screen reader.

## Module 10: Fixing Broken Links 

- Activity type: Video to watch 
- Purpose: This video will go over another big task in the role, fixing broken links on the site. On a monthly basis, we will be given reports for all the links found on the site. A lot of these are external and may need to be reviewed. The video will show all the steps, from organizing your workspace to start, to tutorials on using screaming to find these broken links. It will also show how to manage your process, so that you are able to update these links efficiently, and where to save all these report files (On CSUN Box). 
- Estimated time: ~10-15 mins
- What the learner should understand: The learner should get a clear view of what the task at hand is, and what they must be doing in order to resolve the broken links, document everything, and complete the task. 
- How the learner completes it: Watches video then clicks complete. 
- Accessibility needs: Captions, text on module page must be readable by a screen reader.
